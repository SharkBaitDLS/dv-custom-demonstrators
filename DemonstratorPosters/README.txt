Restoration posters
===================

The museum shows a picture of each demonstrator on the quest board beside its stall.
Put a picture in here to give a locomotive one of your own, or to replace the one included with its mod.

Name the file after the livery ID it belongs to, with a .png extension: <livery-id>.png

The mod's settings menu lists each locomotive under the livery id it uses.
They can be found in the mod's settings menu in brackets after you select the slot for it.
See the LiveryID.jpg in this folder for reference.

Pictures should be square, and 512x512px to be rendered without distortion. The actual displayed
area in-game will be 512x400 due to the name plate at the top, so frame your picture on the assumption
that the top 112px will be covered up with a black bar and text.

Anything you put in here wins over a picture the car's own mod ships.
A locomotive with no picture anywhere simply shows a blank panel.
